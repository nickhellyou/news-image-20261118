One front page summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026: 34 numbered stories across 6 continents, a world map, a "day in numbers" box, and a drawn Gulf hero panel. Lead story: US jets strike Iran as tankers burn in the Strait of Hormuz.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program copies `entry.png` (1024x1024, 151,178 bytes) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`). No network, no dependencies beyond the Python standard library. Two runs produce byte-identical output.

## The page

- **Masthead:** "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Wednesday, 18 November 2026: 34 stories, 6 continents, one front page and a map."
- **Lead:** US-Iran clash in the Gulf — jets hit an IRGC missile site on Qeshm Island after drones targeted a US radar base in Bahrain; Washington says it sank five Iranian tankers; Brent crude at $93.14.
- **34 stories:** Gulf (5), Europe (6), Asia (6), Americas (2), markets & tech (6), climate (4), sport (4) — from the Kerch Bridge strike and the Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral to Nvidia's $115.8bn quarter, Cyclone Akvan, COP31, Gaza, Dhoni's IPL retirement and the new James Bond.
- **Map + numbers:** schematic world map with numbered story markers; Brent $93.14, Nvidia +103%, 146,000 marching in France, 72 dead in Komyshuvakha, 5 tankers sunk, 825m Anthropic IPO shares.
- **Method:** researched from live web sources on 18 Nov 2026 (Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, The Daily Short, Nexus Europa); contested claims attributed, figures as reported that day. Composed at build time with PIL and DejaVu fonts, so all text is rendered, never AI-generated lettering.
